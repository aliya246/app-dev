# My Favorite Movie

## Title: Dahil Mahal na Mahal Kita

**Genre:** Drama, Romance  
**Main Cast:** Rico Yan, Claudine Barretto  

---

## Story Overview
*Dahil Mahal na Mahal Kita* is a Filipino romantic drama about two people from different social backgrounds who fall in love. Their relationship faces challenges due to family expectations, personal struggles, and societal differences.

---

## Why I Like It
- Emotional and realistic love story  
- Strong chemistry between the lead actors  
- Shows sacrifices in relationships  
- Classic Filipino movie
